Engineering Rigor

Key Setbacks & Design Pivots

Evidence-based troubleshooting and architecture decisions.

1. Sensor Rate (11.93 Hz) Pivot
  • Challenge: Too slow for high-bandwidth PID to cancel drawing chatter.
  • Design Strategy: Plan a low-frequency pulse PI trim loop; rely on mechanical compliance to absorb vibration.
2. Lead Screw Backlash Pivot
  • Challenge: M4 nut deadband and Z-dependent friction.
  • Solution: Coaxial preload spring (cancels out on load cell in mid-air).
3. Electrical Noise Pivot
  • Challenge: Stepper switching spikes coupling to 24-bit ADC.
  • Solution: Custom optoisolator board for full galvanic isolation.
