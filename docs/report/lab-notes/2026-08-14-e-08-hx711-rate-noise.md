# Lab Note: 2026-08-14 - E-08 HX711 Rate and Noise

## Objective

Measure the installed HX711's actual stationary sample rate and raw-count noise
to select the toolhead force-control filter and correction timing.

## Setup

- Hardware: SparkFun Pro Micro RP2350; HiLetgo HX711; uxcell 300 g load cell.
- HX711: `DT` to GP0, `SCK` to GP1, `VCC` to Pro Micro `3V3`, and `GND` to
  `TOOL_GND`.
- Service UART: DSD TECH SH-U09C2 set to 3.3 V logic; adapter `GND` to
  `TOOL_GND`, `RXD` to GP20, `TXD` to GP21, and adapter VCC disconnected.
- Local toolhead rail: 6 V applied after USB-C upload cable is removed.
- Safety condition: pen clear of the scale; N20/DRV8833 never enabled by this
  sketch; mechanism must remain stationary during each 15-second run.

## Procedure

1. Turn off local 6 V and upload the E-08 sketch through Pro Micro USB-C.
2. Disconnect USB-C, attach the UART adapter, and open its port at 115200.
3. Turn on local 6 V and confirm the E-08 startup text.
4. Keep the pen clear and stationary, then send `r`.
5. Record the complete `E-08 result` block.
6. Repeat once without changing wiring or pen position.

## Results

Both 15-second runs were performed with the pen clear and stationary:

```text
Run 1
samples=179
window_s=15.000
sample_rate_hz=11.933
mean_counts=112287.2
min_counts=112137
max_counts=112437
peak_to_peak_counts=300
stddev_counts=69.1

Run 2
samples=179
window_s=15.000
sample_rate_hz=11.933
mean_counts=112515.6
min_counts=112326
max_counts=112810
peak_to_peak_counts=484
stddev_counts=120.5
```

The matching 179-sample totals establish an installed rate of about **11.93
Hz**. The second window's 228-count mean shift and 484-count peak-to-peak span
are small relative to the E-07B contact residuals (about 96,000-124,000
counts), but they confirm that a short filter is appropriate.

## Pass criteria

- Both runs report valid samples and a similar measured sample rate.
- Noise is quantified in raw counts, allowing an evidence-based filter and
  deadband choice.

**Passed.** Use three ready samples (about 0.25 s) for the median stage and
do not issue force corrections faster than about 4 Hz, after the actuator's
settling delay. The 50,000-count E-07B residual contact threshold is over 100
times the measured stationary peak-to-peak noise; mechanism preload, not ADC
noise, remains the dominant control uncertainty.

## Follow-up

Use E-08 results to set the control loop's sample grouping and correction
interval. Do not choose PID/PI bandwidth from an assumed HX711 rate.
