the current geometry and safe operating envelope.

pen-clearance, or current measurement was performed as part of this record.

## Interpretation

0.1885 in pen-tip clearance were specific to the removed spring and are

used the previous spring.

length at candidate LIFT/contact positions, and the safe compression margin.

geometry is measured. Then repeat the loaded actuator and force-envelope tests
