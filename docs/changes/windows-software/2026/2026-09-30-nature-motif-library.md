# Nature motif library for the kaleidoscope generator

## Summary

`motifs/nature/` now ships 110 black-on-white nature silhouettes (about 125 KB
total, 1-bit PNG) plus `tools/make_nature_motifs.py` to redraw them or extend
the set. They are the source artwork for the Kaleidoscope Converter's
`Motif folder...` mode.

## Reason

The operator asked for "a motif folder with something like 100 black and white
pngs of nature" to feed the motif mode added in `WSW-20260930-010`.

## Implementation

- `tools/make_nature_motifs.py` draws every shape from code in normalised
  coordinates: leaves (simple, oak, maple, ginkgo, fern, palm, heart/clover),
  flowers (daisy, sunflower, tulip, lotus, bell, hibiscus, dandelion,
  lavender), trees (conifer, round, palm, cypress), sea life (fish, jellyfish,
  starfish, nautilus, fan shell, cowrie, crab, seahorse, octopus, whale, coral,
  waves), birds and insects (flying bird, feather, owl, butterfly, dragonfly,
  bee), small animals (snail, turtle, rabbit, cat, elephant, hedgehog), and
  loose nature (mushroom, acorn, pinecone, mountain, sun, moon, snowflake,
  grass, water drop). Variants take the catalogue to 110 files.
  - Each image is drawn at 4x, downsampled with LANCZOS and thresholded to 1-bit
    so the result is pure black on pure white with no grey fringe for the tracer
    to guess at.
  - `--out` writes elsewhere, `--limit N` makes a quick preview sheet.
- `motifs/README.md` explains how to point the app at the folder, how the motif
  mode behaves, and how to add a shape.
- `software/README.md` points at the shipped folder and the generator script.
- While shipping the set, motif mode's separator bundles were thinned
  (`level // 3` instead of `level`): at full strength they grew into heavy rings
  that swallowed the natural shapes they were supposed to frame.

## Verification

- `python tools\make_nature_motifs.py` writes 110 files, 125 KB total.
- Contact sheets inspected: `samples/png/motif_sheet.png` (all 110) and the
  focus sheets `motif_focus2.png` / `motif_focus3.png`. Shapes that rendered
  badly in the first pass were reworked - ginkgo, bell, lavender, maple - and
  deer/fox were replaced with mushroom, heart leaf, cowrie shell and water drop
  because their silhouettes never read clearly at motif scale.
- Headless app run against the shipped folder: seeds 4/11/23 at intricacy 8 and
  4/11/42 at intricacy 10 build in 0.4-0.9 s, tracing 9-18 of the 110 images
  (only the ones the seed places) and producing 2,400-5,800 mirrored contours;
  `samples/png/gen_nature_motifs2.png` is the after-thinning render.
- `python tools\docs_index.py --write` / `--check` pass.

## Struggles and rejected approaches

- Generating the silhouettes with an image model was rejected: the plotter needs
  crisp, pure black-and-white edges and a deterministic, re-runnable set, which
  code-drawn shapes give and a diffusion model does not.
- First-pass silhouettes for deer, fox and turtle read as insects or blobs at
  motif scale. Rather than iterate indefinitely on animal anatomy, the weakest
  three were replaced with shapes that draw reliably (mushroom, heart leaf,
  cowrie shell, water drop).

## Risks and follow-up

- The library is a starting set, not a curated botanical reference; shapes with
  fine detail (coral, dandelion) can read as texture when a seed packs them
  tightly. Adding a `Motif scale` control is the obvious next step.
- Committing 110 binaries adds about 125 KB to the repository. They are
  regenerated by a tracked script, so they can be rebuilt or dropped later
  without losing anything.

## Files

- `tools/make_nature_motifs.py`: the shape catalogue and renderer.
- `motifs/nature/*.png`: 110 generated silhouettes.
- `motifs/README.md`: usage and extension notes.
