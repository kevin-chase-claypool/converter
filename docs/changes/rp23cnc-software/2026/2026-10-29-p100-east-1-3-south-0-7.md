# Correct P100 registration after the east overshoot

## Summary

The P100 axis-centre correction changes from X `1.45`, Y `1.01` to X `2.75`,
Y `0.31`. This is a net move of 1.3 mm east and 0.7 mm south from the prior
registration frame.

## Reason

The operator requested a direct correction from the current registered pen
position. A 2.3 mm east adjustment overshot, so the final request is to return
1.0 mm west. The final motion is therefore +1.3 mm in machine X and -0.7 mm in
machine Y.

P100 writes its G54 registration using:

```text
G10 L20 P1 X[[sensor_to_pen_x - axis_center_correction_x]]
             Y[[sensor_to_pen_y - axis_center_correction_y]]
```

Increasing the correction moves the registered pen position east/north;
decreasing it moves the pen west/south. Therefore:

```text
axis_center_correction_x = 3.75 - 1.00 = 2.75
axis_center_correction_y = 1.01 - 0.70 = 0.31
```

At the surveyed centroid, the registration write becomes:

```text
G10 L20 P1 X-2.75 Y-29.7992
```

## Implementation

- Updated the two `axis_center_correction_x/y` constants in `P100.macro`.
- Kept the measured `sensor_to_pen` vector unchanged at `(0.0, -29.4892)`.
- Updated the static P100 safety-validator expectations.
- Updated the current-state firmware registration documentation.

## Verification

The macro parser and pinned P100 safety validation must pass before upload.
The requested movement is not itself proof of correct physical registration;
after deployment, run `G65 P113` and verify the parked pen lands 1.3 mm east
and 0.7 mm south of the prior registration check, then confirm the bed can
rotate without moving the pen tip relative to its target.

Local P100 source is 15,448 bytes with SHA-256
`9C944D1922E3DB9808661F43E7F27E80B7B232D40779D7A2EED340002A0776FD`.

## Struggles and rejected approaches

The operator's phrase "pen offset" can refer either to the measured
`sensor_to_pen` geometry or to the registered G54 origin at bed center.
Changing `sensor_to_pen_y` by the requested delta would also move the pen, but
would corrupt the measured physical TMAG-to-pen geometry and repeat the prior
ownership ambiguity. The adjustment therefore belongs in
`axis_center_correction`, consistent with the established registered-jog
workflow.

## Risks and follow-up

The requested correction is a translation-only adjustment. It does not correct
A-axis runout, bed plane variation, scaling, or a rotated pen mount. Re-run the
center-magnet and rotation checks after deployment.
